# The prompt

Sixth game in the harsh-critic-loop series (after
[NEONOID](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neonoid),
[NEON MINER](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neonminer),
[NEODROID](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neodroid),
[NEON DASH](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neondash) and
[NEONLINGS](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neonlings)). After levels
became theorems, the next question was obvious: what happens when there are
no levels — only two wills and a landscape? **Gods as theorems.**

```
Build a Populous tribute at the level of a modern commercial god-game.
Single-file browser game, zero assets. Original world and creatures — the
real Populous (Bullfrog, 1989) is copyrighted. Isometric deformable
terrain raised and lowered one vertex at a time, settlements that grow
with the flat land around them, mana born of worship, the papal magnet,
knights, swamp, earthquake, flood, and armageddon — against an opposing
AI god bound by the same rules.

One owner writes the whole game. Then /loop harsh sub-agent critics: the
three visual lenses plus a POPULOUS-FIDELITY critic judging mechanics
against the 1989 canon.

The harness requirement is absolute: the game's claims are proven by
deterministic god-bot matches, headlessly, every build. The full bot must
WIN its authored match; a god who never acts must LOSE; each pillar
ablated (terraforming, attack powers) must show its weight in the
outcome; and every canon mechanism (flat land grows settlements,
deformation collapses them, the magnet commands the faithful) gets an
isolated machine-checked proof. Grade against the commercial bar, call
out fixes that didn't land in the pixels, loop until plateau, report the
honest number.
```
